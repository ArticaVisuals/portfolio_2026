### Positioning line

> "A brand designer who thinks in systems — and the range is the proof."

This is the one thing the primary ICP wants a hiring reader to remember. Range
is evidence of a systems mind, never the headline.

### The central tension

Range is simultaneously the strongest asset and the #2 reason a hiring lead
passes. A generalist has range and no center. A systems designer has range
because of a center. The portfolio must prove range is a consequence, not a
menu. If a reviewer remembers "range" the portfolio lost. If they remember
"systems thinker whose range proves it" the portfolio won.

### Primary ICP reader

A skeptical-but-hopeful senior design lead at an in-house high-craft team.
Opens a link Micah sent directly. Has 10–20 seconds on the homepage, 3–5
minutes total. Scans three questions:

1. Can this person actually do the work? (Craft bar)
2. Did they really do what they claim? (Attribution)
3. Would I want them in the room? (Taste, judgment, collaboration)

### Top four pass-reasons (candidate's own ranking)

1. "I can't tell what they specifically did." (Attribution)
2. "This reads as a generalist who'll need narrowing." (Focus)
3. "The work isn't at the craft bar yet." (Curation)
4. "Polished but I can't see how they think." (Lowest risk)

### The keep-or-cut test

Does this make craft, attribution, or point of view clearer? Keep.
Does it make the range read as a menu rather than a consequence? Cut.
Is the site outshining the work? Dial the site down.
Is it performing seniority the work hasn't earned? Cut.

### Gaps to address in interview

1. **Years of experience:** The 4–6 year requirement may not match timeline if graduation is recent. Candidate should be prepared to frame timeline honestly.
2. **Marketing-specific work:** Decagon wants marketing web, campaigns, decks. Portfolio evidence is strongest in brand identity and event motion, not marketing campaigns. Optro keynotes and Motion Connect collateral are the closest matches.
3. **Evolving vs. building:** Most evidence is building new systems. Optro's "translating the visual identity into motion rules" is the strongest "evolve" evidence.
4. **San Francisco location:** Candidate is in Pasadena per portfolio. Relocation willingness is unverified.
5. **People management:** Title says "Lead" but body says "Designer." If the role involves managing others, the portfolio shows collaboration (Apple team, Optro stakeholders) but not direct reports.
6. **Figma and Adobe:** Framer is a separate tool from Figma. Neither Figma nor Adobe proficiency is verified by the selected evidence. Ask for specific tool/workflow examples.

### Testable criteria for portfolio review (Decagon lens)

When reviewing portfolio copy against this role:

1. Does the opening paragraph make the candidate's specific contribution clear within 10 seconds?
2. Does the copy demonstrate systems thinking (rules, guidelines, tokens, shared language) rather than one-off execution?
3. Is velocity/shipping evidence present without sacrificing craft claims?
4. Are AI-tool references qualified and honest (curiosity, not expertise)?
5. Does the brand-strategy evidence feel grounded in real constraints or business context?
