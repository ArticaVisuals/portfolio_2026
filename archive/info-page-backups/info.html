info

I'm a brand designer in Los Angeles with a systems mind. I care about the strategic side of design, figuring out what a brand needs to say before deciding how it looks, and I like doing that work collaboratively, across disciplines.

I'm drawn to the spaces where technology, product, and culture overlap. Outdoor and lifestyle brands have always been close to me, but so has the challenge of making complex, technical products feel human. Outside of design, I'm usually training for a marathon, out on the trails, or on a rock wall somewhere.
