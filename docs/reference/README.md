# Portfolio strategy and copy

These documents explain the audience, positioning, and writing decisions behind
Micah Hoang's portfolio.

## Start with the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**[Read the ICP (PDF)](Micah-Portfolio-ICP.pdf)** ·
[Download the Word document](Micah-Portfolio-ICP.docx)

The primary reader is a senior brand or design lead evaluating the quality of
the work, Micah's specific contribution, and his judgment and approach to
collaboration. The ICP connects that reader's concerns to project selection,
attribution, tone of voice, and how much attention the site itself should demand.

Its guiding idea is a brand designer who thinks in systems, with range as the
proof. Secondary perspectives include studio creative directors, startup
founders and design leads, and fellow designers who explore beyond the homepage.
